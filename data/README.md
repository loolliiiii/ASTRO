# NASA Open Data used in ASTRO

ASTRO does not download data while it runs. We read NASA's open sources, picked the facts each level needs, checked them, and built them into the game. `mission_data.json` lists the facts and coordinates used in the game.

| # | NASA dataset / resource | Link | What we took from it | Where it appears in ASTRO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (NSSDCA) | https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov | Launch and landing dates, landing sites, vehicle specs | Robot cards, mission timeline |
| 2 | Mars Exploration Rovers: Spirit & Opportunity | https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-exploration-rovers-spirit-and-opportunity/ | Landing coordinates (Gusev Crater, Meridiani Planum), 90-day design life, science goals (water, hematite), routes and discoveries | Mars Twins level: briefing globe, driving stages, mission cards |
| 3 | Lunar Roving Vehicle (Apollo 15) | NSSDCA ID 1971-063C | Vehicle facts for the "moon buggies" left on the Moon | Next Moon level (planned) |
| 4 | Sojourner, Viking 1 & 2, Ingenuity mission pages | https://science.nasa.gov | Working life and mission summaries | Robot cards, future levels |
| 5 | NASA History Office | https://www.nasa.gov/history/ | Inventory of objects left on the Moon (796 items) | Problem statement, pitch deck |

Challenge resources tab: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, "Abandoned but not Forgotten").

Non-NASA reference: Kilic, C. (West Virginia University, 2022), estimate of 7,119 kg of human-made material on Mars.
